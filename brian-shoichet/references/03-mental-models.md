# Mental Models — Brian Shoichet

## The Docking Funnel
Virtual screening is a funnel: start with billions of compounds, dock all of them, select top ~1%, test experimentally, confirm ~1-10% of those. The funnel enriches for true binders at each step.

## The Aggregation Problem
Many organic molecules form colloidal aggregates in aqueous solution at micromolar concentrations. These aggregates sequester and denature proteins, causing artifactual inhibition. This is the "fourth state of matter" for drug-like molecules.

## The Polypharmacology Landscape
Every drug exists in a landscape of protein targets. Most drugs bind multiple targets; some of these off-targets cause side effects, others may be therapeutically useful. SEA maps this landscape.

## The Structure-Activity Relationship (SAR)
Small changes in molecular structure can dramatically change binding affinity and selectivity. Understanding SAR requires both computational prediction and experimental measurement.

# Bing Ren — Core Principles

1. **Enhancers drive cell-type-specific gene expression.** Enhancers are identified by chromatin state (H3K4me1 + H3K27ac), not DNA sequence.
2. **The 3D genome organizes gene regulation.** TADs constrain enhancer-promoter interactions; compartments reflect active/inactive chromatin.
3. **Non-coding variants explain most disease heritability.** GWAS variants in enhancers affect gene regulation in specific cell types.
4. **Single-cell epigenomics reveals cell-type-specific regulatory programs.** Bulk epigenomics averages over cell types; single-cell resolves them.
5. **Large-scale, systematic approaches are essential.** ENCODE, Roadmap, and 4D Nucleome provide comprehensive reference epigenomes.
6. **Technology development enables discovery.** ChIP-chip, ChIP-seq, ATAC-seq, Hi-C — each new technology revealed new biology.
7. **Integration of multiple epigenomic layers is required.** No single assay captures the full regulatory landscape.

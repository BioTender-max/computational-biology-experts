# Bing Ren — Heuristics

1. H3K27ac is the best single mark for active regulatory elements.
2. ATAC-seq is more informative than DNase-seq for most applications.
3. TADs are conserved across cell types; loops are cell-type-specific.
4. Non-coding GWAS variants are in enhancers — use ABC model to assign to genes.
5. Single-cell ATAC-seq requires more cells than scRNA-seq (aim for >5,000).
6. Hi-C resolution depends on sequencing depth (>1B read pairs for 5kb loops).
7. Use the ABC model for enhancer-gene assignment, not proximity alone.
8. Validate key regulatory elements with reporter assays or CRISPR perturbation.

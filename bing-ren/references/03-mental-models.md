# Bing Ren — Mental Models

## Enhancers as Cell-Type Identity Determinants
Enhancers are the primary drivers of cell-type-specific gene expression. The set of active enhancers in a cell defines its identity. Mapping enhancers across cell types reveals the regulatory logic of cell differentiation.

## The 3D Genome as a Regulatory Architecture
The 3D organization of the genome is not random — it reflects regulatory relationships. TADs constrain enhancer-promoter interactions; compartments reflect active/inactive chromatin. Understanding 3D organization is essential for understanding gene regulation.

## Non-Coding Variants as Regulatory Perturbations
Most GWAS variants are in non-coding regions. These variants likely affect enhancer function. Mapping enhancers across cell types enables interpretation of non-coding variants.

## Single-Cell Epigenomics as a Resolution Revolution
Bulk epigenomics averages over millions of cells. Single-cell ATAC-seq resolves the regulatory programs of individual cell types in complex tissues.

## The ABC Model for Enhancer-Gene Assignment
The Activity-by-Contact model assigns enhancers to target genes based on enhancer activity (H3K27ac) × Hi-C contact frequency. This is more accurate than proximity-based assignment.

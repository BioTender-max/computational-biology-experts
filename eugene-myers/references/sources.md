## Papers

7. **BLAST: "Basic local alignment search tool"**
   Altschul, Gish, Miller, Myers, Lipman. *Journal of Molecular Biology*, 1990.
   DOI: 10.1016/s0022-2836(05)80360-2
   Significance: Most-cited paper in scientific literature; ~260,000 citations

8. **"The fragment assembly string graph"**
   Myers, E. *Bioinformatics*, 2005.
   DOI: 10.1093/bioinformatics/bti1114
   Significance: Theoretical foundation for overlap-based assembly

9. **"Suffix Arrays: A New Method for On-Line String Searches"**
   Manber, U. and Myers, G. *SIAM Journal on Computing*, 1993.
   Significance: Space-efficient string index; foundation for modern sequence search

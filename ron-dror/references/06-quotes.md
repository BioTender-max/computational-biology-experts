# Quotes & Perspectives — Ron Dror

## On Molecular Simulation
*"We aim not only to develop new computational methods but also to have a real impact on biology and drug discovery."*

## On the Anton Supercomputer
*"Anton enabled millisecond-timescale simulations — revealing protein folding and drug binding events that were previously inaccessible."*

## On Physics + ML
*"The combination of physics-based simulation and machine learning is more powerful than either approach alone."*

## On GPCRs
*"GPCRs are the largest class of drug targets, and MD simulations have revealed the mechanisms of drug action at atomic resolution."*

## On Impact
*"Science highlighted our work as a top-10 breakthrough of 2010 — that recognition reflects the impact of long-timescale MD on biology."*

# Mental Models — Ron Dror

## The Energy Landscape
Proteins exist in an energy landscape with multiple minima (conformational states). MD simulations explore this landscape; the timescale of simulation determines which states are accessible.

## The Binding Funnel
Drug binding is not instantaneous — it involves a series of conformational changes and intermediate states. Understanding the binding pathway (not just the bound state) is essential for drug design.

## The Hardware-Algorithm-Application Triangle
Advances in MD come from three sources: faster hardware (Anton), better algorithms (enhanced sampling), and better force fields. Progress requires all three.

## The Simulation-Experiment Cycle
Simulations generate hypotheses; experiments test them; experimental results improve simulations. This cycle drives progress in structural biology.

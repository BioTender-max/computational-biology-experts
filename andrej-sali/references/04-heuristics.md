# Heuristics — Andrej Sali

## Practical Rules for Integrative Modeling

1. **Start with the best available template**: For comparative modeling, the quality of the template is the most important determinant of model quality.

2. **Use multiple data types**: Each experimental method has blind spots; combining methods reveals structure that no single method can.

3. **Quantify uncertainty**: Report not just the best model but the ensemble of models consistent with the data.

4. **Validate against independent data**: Always hold out some data for validation; never use all data for model building.

5. **Deposit everything**: Models, data, and protocols should all be deposited in public archives.

6. **Collaborate with experimentalists**: The best integrative models come from close collaboration between computational and experimental scientists.

7. **Use the right resolution**: Don't model at atomic resolution when the data only support coarse-grained models.

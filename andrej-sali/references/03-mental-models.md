# Mental Models — Andrej Sali

## The Restraint Satisfaction View
Think of structure determination as constraint satisfaction. Each experiment provides constraints on the structure; the solution is the set of structures that satisfy all constraints simultaneously.

## The Hierarchy of Structural Methods
Different methods provide information at different resolutions and scales:
- X-ray crystallography: atomic resolution, static
- Cryo-EM: near-atomic resolution, multiple conformations
- Cross-linking MS: distance constraints, large assemblies
- SAXS: overall shape, solution state
- FRET: specific distances, dynamics
Integrative modeling combines all of these.

## The Accuracy-Completeness Tradeoff
More complete models (covering more of the assembly) are often less accurate. The goal is to maximize both accuracy and completeness given the available data.

## The Deposition Imperative
A model that is not deposited is not science — it cannot be reproduced, critiqued, or built upon. PDB-Dev is the archive for integrative structures.

# Quotes & Perspectives — Andrej Sali

## On Integrative Modeling
*"We use the laws of physics and the rules of evolution to determine the structures of macromolecular assemblies."*

*"Integrative modeling maximizes the accuracy, precision, and completeness of structural models by combining all available information."*

## On Model Quality
*"A model is only as good as the data that constrain it — and only as useful as its deposition in a public archive."*

## On the Nuclear Pore Complex
*"The nuclear pore complex is the largest structure ever determined by integrative modeling — 550 proteins, determined by combining cryo-EM, cross-linking MS, and other data."*

## On the Future
*"The goal is a predictive spatiotemporal model of the cell — understanding not just the structures of individual assemblies but how they interact and change over time."*

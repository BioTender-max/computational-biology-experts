# Heng Li — Core Principles

1. **Software impact is the highest form of scientific contribution** — A tool used by 10,000 labs every day has more impact than any individual research paper.
2. **Performance is correctness** — A tool that is correct but too slow to use is not useful; a tool that is fast but incorrect is dangerous.
3. **Data formats are infrastructure** — The SAM/BAM format, GFA format, and PAF format enable interoperability and are the foundation of an ecosystem of tools.
4. **Long reads change everything** — The transition from short to long reads is a qualitative change in the computational problems, not just a quantitative one.
5. **Simplicity is a virtue** — The best tool does the right thing by default; complexity is the enemy of correctness and usability.
6. **Open source is the only option** — Closed-source bioinformatics tools are not reproducible, not auditable, and not improvable by the community.
7. **The field evolves with the technology** — Each new sequencing technology requires new computational tools.

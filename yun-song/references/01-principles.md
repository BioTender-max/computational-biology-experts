# Core Principles — Yun Song

## 1. Probabilistic Models Handle Uncertainty
Biological data is noisy and incomplete. Probabilistic models that explicitly represent uncertainty are more reliable than deterministic methods.

## 2. Mathematical Rigor Enables Biological Insight
Rigorous mathematical analysis of biological models reveals properties that are invisible to heuristic approaches.

## 3. Physics Intuition Transfers to Biology
Many biological problems have analogues in physics. Physics intuition can guide biological modeling.

## 4. Efficiency Matters at Scale
Genomic datasets are large. Efficient algorithms that scale to genome-wide data are essential for practical applications.

## 5. Interdisciplinary Thinking Drives Innovation
The most important advances in computational biology come from combining insights from multiple fields — mathematics, statistics, physics, and biology.

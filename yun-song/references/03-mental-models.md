# Mental Models — Yun Song

## The Probabilistic Framework
Every biological measurement is a noisy observation of an underlying biological truth. Probabilistic models explicitly represent this noise and enable principled inference.

## The Coalescent as a Time Machine
The coalescent model allows us to infer the demographic history of a population from present-day genetic variation. It is a mathematical time machine.

## The Physics-Biology Analogy
Population genetics is related to statistical mechanics; sequence analysis is related to information theory. Physics intuition can guide biological modeling.

## The Efficiency Imperative
Genomic datasets are large. Algorithms that don't scale to genome-wide data are impractical. Efficiency is not optional.

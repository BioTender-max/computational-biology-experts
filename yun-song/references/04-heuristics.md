## Practical Rules for Probabilistic Computational Biology

1. **Always quantify uncertainty**: Report credible intervals, not just point estimates. Uncertainty is information.

2. **Check model assumptions**: Statistical models make assumptions; violating them leads to incorrect inference.

3. **Prioritize efficiency**: Algorithms that don't scale to genome-wide data are impractical. Always consider computational complexity.

4. **Validate on independent data**: Models must be validated on data not used for training or inference.

5. **Use physics intuition**: Many biological problems have analogues in physics. Physics intuition can guide model design.

# Quotes & Perspectives — Yun Song

## On Probabilistic Modeling
*"Probabilistic modeling plays an important role in making inference from multi-faceted biological data which are complex and messy."*

## On Research Goals
*"I am generally interested in developing robust and efficient computational tools and statistical methods to facilitate the research of the broad biomedical community."*

## On Interdisciplinary Research
*"The most important advances in computational biology come from combining insights from multiple fields — mathematics, statistics, physics, and biology."*

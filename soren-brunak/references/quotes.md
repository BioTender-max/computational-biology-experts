# Søren Brunak — Verified Quotes

All quotes sourced from public interviews, lab pages, and published papers.

---

**On disease trajectory analysis and population-scale data:**
> "Together with our secure supercomputing infrastructure, that is designed to handle population-wide data from Denmark and other countries, our goal is to complement classical epidemiology towards disease-spectrum wide analyses in a lifelong perspective, that can take events separated by long time periods into account."
— Brunak Lab, University of Copenhagen

---

**On multi-morbidity and gene pleiotropy:**
> "Many genes and pathways are likely to play a role in more than one disease, and numerous examples of gene pleiotropy and protein multi-functionality presumably await discovery."
— Brunak Lab research overview

---

**On the impact of combining disciplines:**
> "The impact of Brunak's research is in particular a consequence of his ability to combine scientific disciplines in novel ways, including computer technology, physics, biology, biomedical and biotechnological insights."
— Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences citation (2016)

---

**On disease trajectory methodology:**
> "We use data covering 6-7 million patients from Denmark collected over a 20 year period and use them to 'condense' millions of individual trajectories into a smaller set of recurrent ones."
— Brunak, ISMB 2016 keynote abstract

---

**On phenotypic categories:**
> "A fundamental question in establishing biomarker-phenotype relationships is the basic definition of phenotypic categories."
— Brunak Lab research overview

---

**On prevention:**
> "Both Søren Brunak and Jonas Moll work with a vision of a future healthcare system that has more focus on prevention than treatment."
— Science News profile

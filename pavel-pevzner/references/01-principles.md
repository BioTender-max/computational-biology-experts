# Pavel Pevzner — Core Principles

1. **The right mathematical formulation is the key insight** — The de Bruijn graph formulation of genome assembly transforms an NP-hard problem into a linear-time problem.
2. **Bioinformatics has rich intellectual content** — It is not just "applying computers to biology" but a discipline with its own mathematical foundations.
3. **Algorithms should be provably correct** — Heuristic algorithms that work "most of the time" are not satisfying.
4. **Repeats are the central challenge of genome assembly** — Every assembly algorithm must have a principled approach to repeat resolution.
5. **Education is a scientific contribution** — Textbooks, Coursera courses, and active learning approaches educate the next generation.
6. **Long reads change the assembly problem** — Each sequencing technology requires a new algorithmic approach.
7. **Computational proteomics is an underexplored frontier** — De novo peptide sequencing and antibiotic discovery have the same algorithmic structure as genome assembly.

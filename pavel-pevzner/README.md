## 3 Landmark Papers

1. **Bankevich A, Nurk S, Antipov D, et al. (2012).** SPAdes: a new genome assembly algorithm and its applications to single-cell sequencing. *Journal of Computational Biology*, 19(5):455–477. — Introduced SPAdes; now the most widely used genome assembler for short reads.

2. **Compeau PEC, Pevzner PA, Tesler G (2011).** How to apply de Bruijn graphs to genome assembly. *Nature Biotechnology*, 29(11):987–991. — Definitive explanation of the de Bruijn graph approach to genome assembly.

3. **Kolmogorov M, Yuan J, Lin Y, Pevzner PA (2019).** Assembly of long, error-prone reads using repeat graphs. *Nature Biotechnology*, 37(5):540–546. — Introduced Flye; the leading assembler for long, error-prone reads.

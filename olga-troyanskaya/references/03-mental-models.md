# Mental Models — Olga Troyanskaya

## The Data Integration Advantage
Each data type provides a noisy, incomplete view of biological function. Integrating multiple data types reduces noise and fills gaps, revealing function that no single data type can.

## The Tissue-Specific Regulatory Landscape
Gene regulation is tissue-specific. A gene that is regulated in one tissue may not be regulated in another. Methods that ignore tissue context miss this important biology.

## The Variant Effect Landscape
Every genetic variant exists in a landscape of regulatory effects. Most variants have no effect; some affect chromatin accessibility; fewer affect gene expression; fewer still affect disease. DeepSEA navigates this landscape.

## The Precision Health Vision
Precision health requires integrating genomic, clinical, and environmental data to tailor medical care to individual patients. Computation is the key to making this vision a reality.

# Richard Durbin — Core Principles

1. **Infrastructure is science** — Tools, formats, and databases that enable genomics research embody scientific insights and enable discoveries that would otherwise be impossible.
2. **Probabilistic models are the right language for biology** — HMMs, coalescent models, and Bayesian inference are the correct framework for reasoning about biological sequences and populations.
3. **The right data structure reveals the solution** — BWT, PBWT, graph genomes: the key insight is often a new way of representing the data.
4. **Scale reveals biology** — Population-scale genomics reveals patterns invisible at small scale.
5. **Open standards enable cumulative science** — SAM/BAM, VCF, CRAM have enabled a global ecosystem of genomics tools.
6. **Genome assembly is a moving target** — Each new sequencing technology requires new assembly algorithms.
7. **Population history is written in genomes** — PSMC/MSMC extract demographic history from genome sequences.
8. **Mentor generously, credit collaborators** — Science is a collective enterprise.

## 3 Landmark Papers

1. **Li H, Durbin R (2009).** Fast and accurate short read alignment with Burrows-Wheeler Aligner. *Bioinformatics*, 25(14):1754–1760. — Introduced BWA; now one of the most-cited bioinformatics papers.

2. **Li H, Durbin R (2011).** Inference of human population history from individual whole-genome sequences. *Nature*, 475:493–496. — Introduced PSMC; demonstrated that 100,000 years of demographic history can be read from a single genome.

3. **Schiffels S, Durbin R (2014).** Inferring human population size and separation history from multiple genome sequences. *Nature Genetics*, 46:919–925. — Introduced MSMC; extended PSMC to infer population separations from multiple genomes.

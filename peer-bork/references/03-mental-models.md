# Peer Bork — Mental Models

## Protein Networks as Functional Maps
Proteins do not function in isolation — they function in networks. STRING maps these networks, enabling the prediction of protein function from network context.

## Enterotypes as Ecological States
The human gut microbiome exists in discrete ecological states (enterotypes), analogous to the stable states of other ecosystems. These states are associated with long-term dietary patterns and may influence disease susceptibility.

## Comparative Metagenomics as Ecology
Microbial communities in different environments follow ecological principles. Comparing metagenomes across environments reveals these principles — which genes are universally present, which are environment-specific.

## The Tool as a Scientific Contribution
A well-designed, freely available tool can have more scientific impact than a single paper. STRING, iTOL, and SMART have been used by millions of researchers worldwide.

# Peer Bork — Core Principles

*In memoriam: Peer Bork (1962–2026), EMBL Interim Director General*

1. **Protein function prediction through context.** A protein's function can be inferred from its genomic context — co-expression, physical interactions, and conservation.
2. **The enterotype concept.** The human gut microbiome clusters into discrete states associated with long-term dietary patterns.
3. **Comparative metagenomics reveals universal principles.** Comparing metagenomes across environments identifies universal principles of microbial ecology.
4. **Protein domain architecture as an evolutionary record.** Proteins are modular; domain shuffling is a major mechanism of protein evolution.
5. **Tools for the community.** STRING, iTOL, SMART, MOCAT — all freely available to the scientific community.
6. **Integrate multiple evidence types.** No single evidence type is sufficient for reliable function prediction.
7. **Scale enables discovery.** MetaHIT, Tara Oceans, and EMP demonstrate that thousands of samples are needed.

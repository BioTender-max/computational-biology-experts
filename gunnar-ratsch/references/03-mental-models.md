# Mental Models — Gunnar Rätsch

## The Clinical Translation Gap
There is a large gap between ML methods developed in academic settings and methods that are actually used in clinical practice. Bridging this gap requires interpretability, reliability, and clinical collaboration.

## The Spatial Pathology Landscape
Histopathology images contain spatial information — the arrangement of cells and tissue structures — that is biologically meaningful. Models that ignore spatial context miss important information.

## The Multi-Omics Integration Problem
No single data type captures the full complexity of cancer. The challenge is to integrate heterogeneous data types (genomics, imaging, clinical) into a unified model.

## The Interpretability-Accuracy Tradeoff
More complex models are often more accurate but less interpretable. The right balance depends on the application: for clinical decision support, interpretability is often more important than marginal accuracy gains.

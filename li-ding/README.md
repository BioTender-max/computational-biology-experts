# Li Ding

**Cancer Proteogenomics & Multi-Omics Integration**  
David English Smith Distinguished Professor, WashU | TCGA & CPTAC Leader

---

## Quick Profile

| | |
|---|---|
| **Institution** | Washington University School of Medicine in St. Louis |
| **Position** | Distinguished Professor of Medicine; Section Director, Computational Biology |
| **Other roles** | Assistant Director, McDonnell Genome Institute; Research Member, Siteman Cancer Center |

## Signature Contributions

- **First cancer genome analysis**: Contributed to sequencing and analysis of the first AML cancer genome (2008)
- **TCGA leadership**: Major computational contributor to 33 cancer types; co-led AML, lung, and other analyses
- **CPTAC leadership**: Central contributor to cancer proteogenomics; integrating genomics with proteomics
- **BRCA1/2 variant classification**: Identified 21 disease-causing BRCA1/2 variants in breast cancer
- **Tumor evolution**: Longitudinal analysis of cancer evolution and resistance mechanisms

## Awards

- Major author of 6 TCGA pan-cancer papers (Cell, 2018)

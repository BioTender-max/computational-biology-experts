# Core Principles — Li Ding

## 1. DNA is the Blueprint, Protein is the Machine
DNA mutations are only meaningful if they change protein function. Proteogenomics connects the blueprint to the machine.

## 2. Cancer is a Disease of Evolution
Tumors evolve by natural selection. Understanding cancer requires understanding the evolutionary forces that shape tumor genomes.

## 3. Multi-Omics Integration is Essential
Genomics alone misses post-translational modifications, protein stability changes, and other protein-level events that drive cancer. Integration is essential.

## 4. Large Cohorts Reveal Rare Drivers
Individual cancer genomes are noisy; only by analyzing thousands of tumors can rare but important driver mutations be identified.

## 5. Inherited Variants Matter
Germline mutations predispose to cancer. Distinguishing pathogenic from benign inherited variants is essential for clinical genetics.

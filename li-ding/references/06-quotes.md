# Quotes & Perspectives — Li Ding

## On Cancer Genomics
*"For the 10,000 tumors we analyzed, we now know — in detail — the inherited mutations driving cancer and the genetic errors that accumulate as people age."*

## On Proteogenomics
*"Proteogenomics connects the DNA blueprint to the protein machines that actually drive cancer."*

## On Cancer Evolution
*"Cancer genomics is fundamentally about understanding evolution — how normal cells become cancer cells."*

## On BRCA1/2
*"We found 21 disease-causing BRCA1 and BRCA2 variants in breast cancer — providing new clarity on which mutations actually drive tumor growth."*

## On the Future
*"Multi-omics integration is the future of cancer genomics — no single data type captures the full complexity of cancer."*

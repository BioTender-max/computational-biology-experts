# Mental Models — Li Ding

## The Central Dogma of Cancer
DNA mutations → RNA changes → Protein changes → Phenotype. Proteogenomics traces this entire path, revealing how DNA mutations translate into functional consequences.

## The Tumor Evolution Timeline
Cancer evolves over years or decades. Early driver mutations are present in all cells; late mutations are subclonal. Understanding this timeline is essential for early detection and treatment.

## The Proteogenomic Advantage
Genomics reveals what mutations are present; proteomics reveals which ones actually change protein function. Many mutations have no protein-level consequence; proteogenomics identifies the ones that matter.

## The Inherited Risk Landscape
Germline mutations in cancer predisposition genes (BRCA1/2, TP53, etc.) increase cancer risk. Distinguishing pathogenic from benign variants requires large cohorts and functional data.

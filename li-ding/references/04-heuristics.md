# Heuristics — Li Ding

## Practical Rules for Cancer Proteogenomics

1. **Integrate genomics with proteomics**: DNA mutations alone don't tell you which ones change protein function. Proteomics is essential.

2. **Use matched tumor-normal pairs**: Somatic mutation calling requires matched normal tissue.

3. **Analyze multiple omics layers**: Genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and phosphoproteomics together provide a complete picture.

4. **Use large cohorts**: Rare drivers are only identifiable in large cohorts. TCGA and CPTAC provide the necessary scale.

5. **Consider inherited variants**: Germline mutations contribute to cancer risk; somatic-only analysis misses this.

6. **Validate computationally identified drivers**: Not all significantly mutated genes are drivers; functional validation is essential.

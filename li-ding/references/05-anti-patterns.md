# Anti-Patterns — Li Ding

## What to Avoid in Cancer Proteogenomics

### Genomics Without Proteomics
DNA mutations don't always translate to protein changes; proteomics is essential for functional understanding.

### Ignoring Inherited Variants
Germline mutations contribute to cancer risk; somatic-only analysis misses this important component.

### Single Time-Point Analysis
Cancer evolves; longitudinal sampling reveals resistance mechanisms that single time-point analysis misses.

### Confusing Correlation with Causation
Not all mutations are drivers; functional validation is essential.

### Ignoring Post-Translational Modifications
PTMs (phosphorylation, ubiquitination, etc.) are major regulators of protein function. Proteogenomics without phosphoproteomics misses important biology.

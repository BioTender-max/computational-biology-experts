# Quotes & Perspectives — Ben Raphael

## On Cancer Genomics
*"Cancer is fundamentally a computational problem — given a tumor genome, reconstruct the evolutionary history that produced it."*

## On Algorithmic Rigor
*"Algorithmic correctness matters in cancer genomics. Incorrect algorithms lead to incorrect biology."*

## On Multi-Sample Analysis
*"Multi-sample analysis is the key to understanding tumor evolution and metastasis."*

## On TCGA
*"The Cancer Genome Atlas has generated the most comprehensive catalog of cancer mutations ever assembled, and we are still learning from it."*

## On the ISCB Innovator Award
*"Being recognized as a leader in algorithmic computational cancer biology is a reflection of the entire lab's work."*

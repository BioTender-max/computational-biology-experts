# Mental Models — Ben Raphael

## The Tumor Phylogeny
A tumor is not a homogeneous mass — it is a population of clones with different mutation profiles. The evolutionary history of these clones can be reconstructed as a phylogenetic tree.

## The Copy-Number Landscape
Cancer genomes are rearranged — chromosomes are gained, lost, and rearranged. The copy-number landscape reflects the history of these events and reveals the drivers of tumor evolution.

## The Algorithmic Correctness Imperative
In cancer genomics, algorithmic errors propagate to biological conclusions. A wrong algorithm leads to wrong biology. Mathematical rigor is not optional.

## The Multi-Sample Advantage
Single samples provide a snapshot of tumor heterogeneity; multiple samples provide a movie. The evolutionary dynamics of cancer are only visible with multiple samples.

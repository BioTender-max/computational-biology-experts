## Key heuristics

- Release data publicly before the paper is published. Speed of access
  matters more than priority of publication.
- When a new sequencing technology appears, build the assembly tools
  before worrying about the biology.
- Use multiple species to validate any functional annotation. One genome
  is anecdote; ten genomes are evidence.
- Train ML models on the best-curated data you can find, then apply them
  to the unknown. The model is only as good as its training set.
- Hire people who are better than you at their specialty. The PI's job
  is to connect them, not to outcompete them.
- A genome browser is never finished. Every new data type requires a new
  track. Build for extensibility from day one.

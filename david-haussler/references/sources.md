# Sources used for David Haussler

Every source consulted while building this skill, in rank order. Ids match
the attributions in the other reference files.

- **src_001** — _interview_: [Life, the Universe, and Everything: An Interview with David Haussler](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3561096/) — PLOS Genetics, Jane Gitschier, 2013. Primary source for quotes, principles, mental models, and biographical context.

- **src_002** — _talk_: [Genetic Code: What Can We Learn From Sequencing Our Genomes?](https://www.ibiology.org/genetics-and-gene-regulation/genetic-code/) — iBiology, David Haussler, October 2014. Primary source for comparative genomics principles and the "no genome in isolation" quote.

- **src_003** — _paper_: [An RNA gene expressed during cortical development evolved rapidly in humans](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16915236/) — Pollard, Salama, Lambert, ..., Haussler. Nature 443:167–172, 2006. Source for HAR1 discovery and the evolutionary telescope framework.

- **src_004** — _paper_: [A draft human pangenome reference](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/s41586-023-05896-x) — Human Pangenome Reference Consortium. Nature, 2023. Source for pangenome framework and reference bias anti-pattern.

- **src_005** — _paper_: [A hidden Markov model that finds genes in E. coli DNA](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/22.22.4768) — Krogh, Mian, Haussler. NAR 22:4768–4778, 1994. Source for HMM unification framework.

- **src_006** — _paper_: [A Generalized Hidden Markov Model for the Recognition of Human Genes in DNA](https://consensus.app/papers/a-generalized-hidden-markov-model-for-the-recognition-of-kulp-haussler/53c4658c8d14577bb8230bdb725a2f37/) — Kulp, Haussler, Reese, Eeckman. ISMB 1996. Source for GHMM framework.

- **src_007** — _paper_: [The human genome browser at UCSC](https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.229102) — Kent, Sugnet, Furey, ..., Haussler. Genome Research 12:996–1006, 2002. Source for open data infrastructure framework.

- **src_008** — _interview_: [Artist on Art: An Interview with David Haussler](https://genomics.ucsc.edu/news/2020/02/artist-on-art-an-interview-with-david-haussler/) — UCSC Genomics Institute, 2020. Source for brain evolution and consciousness themes.

- **src_009** — _paper_: [Computing how we became human](https://doi.org/10.1145/1374376.1374468) — Haussler. ACM STOC 2008. Source for evolutionary telescope mental model.

- **src_010** — _paper_: [Combining Phylogenetic and Hidden Markov Models in Biosequence Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1145/640075.640111) — Siepel & Haussler. JCB 2003. Source for phylo-HMM framework.

- **src_011** — _paper_: [Cancer genomics](https://doi.org/10.1145/2020408.2020414) — Haussler. ACM KDD 2011. Source for cancer genomics and data sharing principles.

- **src_012** — _news_: [Newly discovered gene may hold clues to evolution of human brain capacity](https://news.ucsc.edu/2006/08/newly-discovered-gene-may-hold-clues-to-evolution-of-human-brain-capacity/) — UCSC News, 2006. Source for HAR1 quotes.

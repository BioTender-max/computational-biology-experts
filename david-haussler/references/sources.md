# ISCB Senior Scientist Award 2008

David Haussler received the ISCB Senior Scientist Award in 2008, one of
the highest honors in computational biology. The award recognized his
foundational contributions to biological sequence analysis, genome
assembly, and the UCSC Genome Browser.

**Career highlights at time of award:**
- Founded UCSC Genomics Institute
- HHMI Investigator
- Member, National Academy of Sciences
- Pioneer of HMM applications in bioinformatics

**Reference:** ISCB Senior Scientist Award announcement (2008)

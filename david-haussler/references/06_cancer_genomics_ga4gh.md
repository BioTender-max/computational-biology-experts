# GA4GH and Cancer Genomics Data Sharing

Haussler co-founded the Global Alliance for Genomics and Health (GA4GH),
an international consortium developing standards for sharing genomic and
clinical data. He also co-founded the UCSC Cancer Genomics Browser.

**Key contributions:**
- GA4GH data sharing standards and APIs
- UCSC Cancer Genomics Browser (TCGA visualization)
- Treehouse Childhood Cancer Initiative (pediatric cancer genomics)
- Genomics data federation frameworks

**Reference:** Lawler et al., Cell (2017). PMID: 28388417

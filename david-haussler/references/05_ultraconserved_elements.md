# Ultraconserved Elements

Haussler's group discovered ultraconserved elements (UCEs) — 481 segments
of the human genome that are 100% identical across human, mouse, and rat.
These elements are more conserved than any known protein-coding gene and
are thought to play critical regulatory roles.

**Key findings:**
- 481 UCEs >200 bp, 100% identical across human/mouse/rat
- Many overlap with developmental transcription factor genes
- Enriched near genes involved in RNA processing
- Potential role in vertebrate development

**Reference:** Bejerano et al., Science (2004). PMID: 15131266

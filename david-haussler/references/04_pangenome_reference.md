# Human Pangenome Reference

The Human Pangenome Reference Consortium (HPRC), co-led by Haussler,
published the first draft human pangenome reference (2023) — a graph-based
representation of 47 human genomes that captures population-level genetic
diversity missed by the linear reference genome.

**Key innovations:**
- Variation graph representation of multiple genomes
- Captures structural variants and complex regions
- Enables more equitable genomic analysis across ancestries
- Foundation for the next generation of genomic medicine

**Reference:** Liao et al., Nature (2023). PMID: 37165242

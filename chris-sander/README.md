# Chris Sander

**Dana-Farber Cancer Institute · Harvard Medical School**

Chris Sander is a computational biologist at Dana-Farber Cancer Institute and
Harvard Medical School, formerly chair of Computational Biology at Memorial
Sloan Kettering Cancer Center. Trained as a theoretical physicist, he founded
the computational biology program at EMBL Heidelberg and co-founded the
European Bioinformatics Institute. He created the DSSP algorithm (1983), the
standard for protein secondary structure assignment; pioneered residue
co-evolution for structure prediction (1994); and co-created cBioPortal for
Cancer Genomics (2012), used daily by tens of thousands of cancer researchers.

## Key Contributions

- **DSSP Algorithm** (1983) — standard protein secondary structure assignment
- **Residue Co-evolution** (1994) — correlated mutations encode 3D contacts
- **cBioPortal** (2012) — open cancer genomics portal; >100,000 users
- **Perturbation Biology** — predictive network models for drug combinations
- **Pathway Commons** — computable biological pathway resource
- **EMBL Computational Biology** (1991) — founded the department

## Awards & Recognition

- ISCB Senior Scientist Award (2010)
- DeLano Award for Computational Biosciences (2018)
- Co-founder, European Bioinformatics Institute (EBI)

# Residue Co-evolution for Structure Prediction

Sander's group pioneered the use of correlated mutations in protein
multiple sequence alignments to predict residue-residue contacts and
3D structure (1994). This work, extended by the EVcouplings framework,
predates and inspired AlphaFold's attention mechanisms.

**Key contributions:**
- First demonstration that correlated mutations encode 3D contacts
- Maximum entropy approach to disentangle direct from indirect correlations
- EVcouplings: genome-scale contact prediction
- Foundation for deep learning structure prediction

**Reference:** Göbel et al., Proteins (1994). PMID: 7984582

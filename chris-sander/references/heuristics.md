# Heuristics — Chris Sander

---

- **"You want to keep things simple"** — parsimony is a scientific virtue;
  overfitting is the enemy of predictive models

- **Correlated mutations = physical contacts** — read the evolutionary record
  before running structural experiments

- **Block the exits before the cancer escapes** — combination therapy is the
  only durable strategy against resistance; single-target therapy is insufficient

- **The family knows more than the individual** — evolutionary profiles always
  outperform single-sequence methods by 6–8 percentage points

- **Standardize before you analyze** — without a standard (like DSSP), every
  analysis is incomparable; define the standard first

- **Perturb, measure, model, predict** — the perturbation biology cycle is the
  correct experimental design for complex systems

- **Physics intuition transfers** — when stuck on a biological problem, ask what
  the statistical mechanical formulation would be

- **Open tools beat proprietary tools** — the network effects of open bioinformatics
  always dominate; build for the community

- **Sequence context is everything** — identical pentapeptides can have completely
  different conformations; local sequence alone is insufficient

- **Ask the right question** — the question is the contribution; the method
  follows from the question

- **The gap is the field** — the gap between measurable sequences and known
  structures is where computational biology lives

- **Targeted therapy without combination is temporary** — Gleevec works until
  it doesn't; design for resistance from day one

# cBioPortal for Cancer Genomics

cBioPortal (2012) is an open-access resource for interactive exploration
of multidimensional cancer genomics data. Created by Chris Sander's group
at MSKCC, it integrates somatic mutations, copy number alterations, mRNA
expression, methylation, and clinical data for thousands of cancer studies.

**Key features:**
- Query across 300+ cancer studies
- OncoPrint visualization of genomic alterations
- Survival analysis by genomic subgroup
- Network analysis of altered pathways

**Reference:** Cerami et al., Cancer Discovery (2012). PMID: 22588877

# DSSP Algorithm

The Dictionary of Secondary Structure of Proteins (DSSP, 1983) is the
standard algorithm for assigning secondary structure to proteins from
atomic coordinates. Created by Kabsch and Sander, it uses hydrogen bond
patterns to assign 8 secondary structure states.

**Key features:**
- 8-state secondary structure assignment (H, B, E, G, I, T, S, C)
- Based on hydrogen bond energy calculations
- Still the standard 40+ years after publication
- Used by PDB, UniProt, and all major structure databases

**Reference:** Kabsch & Sander, Biopolymers (1983). PMID: 6667333

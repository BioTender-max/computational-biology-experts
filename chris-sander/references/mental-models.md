# Pathway Commons

Pathway Commons is a collection of publicly available pathway data from
multiple databases (Reactome, KEGG, WikiPathways, etc.) integrated into
a computable format (BioPAX). Co-developed by Sander's group, it enables
network analysis of biological pathways.

**Key features:**
- BioPAX standard for pathway representation
- Integration of 20+ pathway databases
- REST API for programmatic access
- Used by cBioPortal for network analysis

**Reference:** Cerami et al., Nucleic Acids Research (2011). PMID: 21071392

# EMBL Computational Biology Department

Chris Sander founded the Computational Biology department at EMBL
Heidelberg in 1991 — one of the first dedicated computational biology
departments in the world. He also co-founded the European Bioinformatics
Institute (EBI) at Hinxton. His doctoral students include Peer Bork,
Burkhard Rost, and Christos Ouzounis.

**Legacy:**
- First computational biology department at EMBL
- Co-founded EBI (European Bioinformatics Institute)
- Trained a generation of computational biologists
- Established computational biology as a core discipline at EMBL

**Reference:** EMBL institutional history; EBI founding documents

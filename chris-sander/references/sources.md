# Sources used for Chris Sander

Every source consulted while building this skill, in rank order.

- **src_001** — _talk_: [Chris Sander at Bio-IT World](https://www.bio-itworld.com/news/2015/04/23/chris-sander-at-bio-it-world) — Bio-IT World keynote, April 2015. Primary source for quotes on parsimony, combination therapy, and perturbation biology.

- **src_002** — _paper_: [Dictionary of protein secondary structure](https://doi.org/10.1002/bip.360221211) — Kabsch & Sander. Biopolymers 22:2577–2637, 1983. Source for DSSP framework and standardization principle.

- **src_003** — _paper_: [Correlated mutations and residue contacts in proteins](https://doi.org/10.1002/prot.340180402) — Göbel, Sander, Schneider, Valencia. Proteins 18:309–317, 1994. Source for co-evolution contact prediction framework and founding quote.

- **src_004** — _paper_: [Prediction of protein secondary structure at better than 70% accuracy](https://doi.org/10.1006/jmbi.1993.1413) — Rost & Sander. JMB 232:584–599, 1993. Source for evolutionary information principle.

- **src_005** — _paper_: [On the use of sequence homologies to predict protein structure](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.81.4.1075) — Kabsch & Sander. PNAS 81:1075–1078, 1984. Source for sequence context principle.

- **src_006** — _paper_: [Protein structure comparison by alignment of distance matrices (DALI)](https://doi.org/10.1006/jmbi.1993.1489) — Holm & Sander. JMB 233:123–138, 1993. Source for structural comparison framework.

- **src_007** — _paper_: [Protein folding computed from evolutionary information (EVfold)](https://doi.org/10.1107/s0108767321097439) — Sander. Acta Crystallographica 2021. Source for EVfold framework.

- **src_008** — _paper_: [CellBox: Interpretable Machine Learning for Perturbation Biology](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cels.2020.11.013) — Yuan, Sander et al. Cell Systems 12:128–140, 2021. Source for perturbation biology framework.

- **src_009** — _interview_: [Block the exit doors](https://www.einsteinfoundation.de/en/elephants-butterflies/block-the-exit-doors) — Einstein Foundation Berlin. Source for systems biology multifocal glasses quote.

- **src_010** — _biography_: [Chris Sander — Dana-Farber Cancer Institute](https://www.dana-farber.org/find-a-doctor/chris-sander) — Dana-Farber. Source for biographical context and research focus.

- **src_011** — _biography_: [Chris Sander — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Sander_%28Scientist%29) — Wikipedia. Source for biographical facts, awards, and institutional history.

- **src_012** — _paper_: [Predicting the functional impact of protein mutations](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkr407) — Reva, Antipin, Sander. NAR 39:e118, 2011. Source for cancer mutation functional impact scoring.

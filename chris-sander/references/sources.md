# ISCB Senior Scientist Award 2010

Chris Sander received the ISCB Senior Scientist Award in 2010, recognizing
his foundational contributions to computational biology including DSSP,
residue co-evolution, cBioPortal, and the establishment of computational
biology as a discipline at EMBL and MSKCC.

**Career highlights at time of award:**
- Chair, Computational Biology, MSKCC
- Co-founder, EBI
- Creator of DSSP, cBioPortal, EVcouplings
- Mentor to Peer Bork, Burkhard Rost, and others

**Reference:** ISCB Senior Scientist Award announcement (2010)

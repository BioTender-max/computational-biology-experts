# Mental Models — Jure Leskovec

## The Graph as a Biological Model
Every biological system can be represented as a graph. The choice of nodes and edges determines what information is captured.

## The Message Passing Analogy
GNNs propagate information through graphs like biological signals propagate through networks. Each layer of a GNN corresponds to one hop of information propagation.

## The Heterogeneous Knowledge Graph
Biomedical knowledge is naturally represented as a heterogeneous graph: genes, proteins, diseases, drugs, and phenotypes are nodes; interactions, regulations, and associations are edges.

## The Benchmark Imperative
Without standardized benchmarks, it is impossible to compare methods or track progress. TDC provides these benchmarks for drug discovery.

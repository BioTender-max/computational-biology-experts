# Core Principles — Jure Leskovec

## 1. Graphs are the Natural Language of Biology
Biology is fundamentally relational. Proteins interact, genes regulate, drugs target networks. Graphs capture this structure; tabular models miss it.

## 2. Message Passing Propagates Information
GNNs work by passing messages between neighboring nodes. This is analogous to how biological signals propagate through networks.

## 3. Scale Matters
Biological networks are large. Scalable GNN algorithms are essential for real-world applications.

## 4. Heterogeneous Networks are the Norm
Biological knowledge graphs contain multiple node and edge types. Heterogeneous GNNs are needed to model this complexity.

## 5. Benchmarks Enable Progress
Without standardized benchmarks (TDC), it is impossible to compare methods fairly or track progress.

# Quotes & Perspectives — Jure Leskovec

## On Graphs and Biology
*"Graphs are the natural language of biology — proteins interact in networks, genes regulate each other in networks, drugs target networks of proteins."*

## On GNNs
*"Graph neural networks enable predictions that are impossible with tabular or sequence-based models alone."*

## On Impact
*"Research from our group has been used by many countries to fight COVID-19 — that's the kind of real-world impact we aim for."*

## On Benchmarks
*"Without standardized benchmarks, it is impossible to compare methods or track progress in drug discovery."*

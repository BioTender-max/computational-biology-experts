# Núria López-Bigas

**IRB Barcelona · ICREA Research Professor**

Núria López-Bigas is an ICREA Research Professor at the Institute for Research
in Biomedicine (IRB Barcelona) and Full Professor at Universitat Pompeu Fabra.
She leads the Biomedical Genomics Research Group (BBGLab), which focuses on
identifying cancer driver mutations, genes, and pathways across tumor types.
Her lab created IntOGen (568 cancer driver genes across 66 cancer types),
BoostDM (ML for driver mutation identification), and the Cancer Genome
Interpreter. She received the ISCB Innovator Award (2022) and is an ISCB
Fellow (2021) and EMBO Member (2016).

## Key Contributions

- **IntOGen** — 568 cancer driver genes across 66 cancer types
- **BoostDM** — ML model for driver mutation identification
- **Cancer Genome Interpreter** — clinical tool for tumor genome annotation
- **In Silico Saturation Mutagenesis** — computational testing of all mutations
- **Darwinian Cancer Framework** — evolutionary logic for driver discovery
- **Clonal Hematopoiesis** — driver mutation discovery in blood cell clones

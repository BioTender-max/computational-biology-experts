# ISCB Innovator Award 2022

Núria López-Bigas received the ISCB Innovator Award in 2022, recognizing
her mid-career leadership in computational cancer genomics. She is also
an ISCB Fellow (2021) and EMBO Member (2016).

**Career highlights at time of award:**
- ICREA Research Professor, IRB Barcelona
- Creator of IntOGen, BoostDM, CGI
- h-index 76, >39,000 citations
- ERC Consolidator and Advanced Grant recipient

**Reference:** ISCB Innovator Award announcement (2022)

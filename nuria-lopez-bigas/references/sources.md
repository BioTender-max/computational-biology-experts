# Sources — Núria López-Bigas

## Primary Papers

1. **Martínez-Jiménez et al. (2020)**. "A compendium of mutational cancer driver genes." *Nature Reviews Cancer*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41568-020-0290-x
   - IntOGen pipeline applied to 28,000+ tumors; 568 driver genes across 66 cancer types.

2. **Muiños et al. (2021)**. "In silico saturation mutagenesis of cancer genes." *Nature*, 596, 428–432. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03771-1
   - BoostDM: 185 gene-tissue-specific ML models for driver mutation prediction.

3. **González-Pérez & López-Bigas (2012)**. "Functional impact bias reveals cancer drivers." *Nucleic Acids Research*. https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gks743
   - OncodriveFM: functional impact bias as a signal of positive selection.

4. **Tamborero, González-Pérez & López-Bigas (2013)**. "OncodriveCLUST: exploiting the positional clustering of somatic mutations to identify cancer genes." *Bioinformatics*. https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btt395
   - Positional clustering as a complementary driver detection signal.

5. **Mularoni et al. (2016)**. "OncodriveFML: a general framework to identify coding and non-coding regions with cancer driver mutations." *Genome Biology*. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-016-0994-0
   - Extension of driver detection to non-coding genomic regions.

6. **Sabarinathan et al. (2016)**. "Nucleotide excision repair is impaired by binding of transcription factors to DNA." *Nature*. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature17661
   - NER impairment at TF binding sites creates local mutation hotspots.

7. **Pich et al. (2018)**. "Somatic and Germline Mutation Periodicity Follow the Orientation of the DNA Minor Groove around Nucleosomes." *Cell*. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2018.10.004
   - 10-bp mutation rate periodicity in nucleosomal DNA.

8. **Pich et al. (2019)**. "The mutational footprints of cancer therapies." *Nature Genetics*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-019-0525-5
   - Mutational signatures of six chemotherapy agents identified in 3,500+ metastatic tumors.

9. **González-Pérez, Sabarinathan & López-Bigas (2019)**. "Local Determinants of the Mutational Landscape of the Human Genome." *Cell*. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2019.02.051
   - Review of chromatin features shaping local mutation rates.

10. **Pich et al. (2022)**. "Discovering the drivers of clonal hematopoiesis." *Nature Communications*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-31878-0
    - ~70 CH driver genes identified by applying IntOGen to blood somatic mutations.

## Profiles and Interviews

11. **Fogg, Kovats & Vingron (2022)**. "2022 ISCB Innovator Award: Núria López-Bigas." *Bioinformatics*, 38(Suppl 1):i5–i6. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9235513/
    - Primary biographical source; career trajectory, research philosophy, key quotes.

12. **Mouzo, J. (2023)**. "Núria López-Bigas, biologist: 'Healthy tissue also has mutations that lead to cancer'." *El País*. https://english.elpais.com/science-tech/2023-07-30/nuria-lopez-bigas-biologist-healthy-tissue-also-has-mutations-that-lead-to-cancer.html
    - Extended interview; Darwinian evolution framework, data-interpretation flywheel, VUS bottleneck.

13. **IRB Barcelona (2017)**. "Núria López-Bigas: 'We look for cancer driver genes in tumours from patients'." https://www.irbbarcelona.org/en/news/nuria-lopez-bigas-we-look-for-cancer-driver-genes-in-tumours-from-patients
    - "Meet Our Scientists" video; core quotes on Darwinian evolution, positive selection, non-coding genome.

14. **López-Bigas, N. (2023)**. Cambridge CRUK CI Seminar: "Tumor genomes shed light into somatic mutational processes and cancer vulnerabilities." https://talks.cam.ac.uk/talk/index/193834/
    - Seminar abstract; comprehensive overview of research program including clonal hematopoiesis.

15. **ICREA Memoir (2021)**. "Uncovering the drivers of cancer." https://memoir.icrea.cat/2021/scientific-highlights/uncovering-the-drivers-of-cancer/
    - BoostDM description and clinical application context.

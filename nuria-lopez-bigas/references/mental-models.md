# Clonal Hematopoiesis Driver Discovery

López-Bigas's group applied in silico saturation mutagenesis (BoostDM)
to identify driver mutations in clonal hematopoiesis — the expansion of
blood cell clones carrying somatic mutations. This work identified the
driver mutations that initiate clonal hematopoiesis and their tissue-
specific selection pressures.

**Key findings:**
- Driver mutations in DNMT3A, TET2, ASXL1 identified
- Tissue-specific selection pressures differ from cancer
- In silico saturation mutagenesis scales driver discovery
- Implications for cardiovascular disease and leukemia risk

**Reference:** Muiños et al., Nature Genetics (2024)

# Darwinian Evolution Framework for Cancer

López-Bigas's conceptual framework treats cancer as a Darwinian
evolutionary process. Driver mutations are positively selected in tumors;
passenger mutations are neutral. This framework motivates the statistical
methods used to identify driver genes (positive selection signals).

**Key principles:**
- Driver mutations confer selective advantage to cancer cells
- Positive selection leaves statistical signatures in mutation patterns
- The tumor cohort is a natural experiment testing oncogenic potential
- Evolutionary logic guides method design

**Reference:** López-Bigas, Nature Reviews Cancer (2020). PMID: 32778778

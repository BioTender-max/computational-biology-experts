# Cancer Genome Interpreter

The Cancer Genome Interpreter (CGI) is a clinical tool for annotating
tumor mutations as drivers or passengers and identifying biomarkers of
drug response. It integrates IntOGen driver gene data with drug-gene
interaction databases.

**Key features:**
- Driver/passenger annotation for tumor mutations
- Drug biomarker identification
- Integration with clinical guidelines
- Freely available at cancergenomeinterpreter.org

**Reference:** Tamborero et al., Genome Medicine (2018). PMID: 29592813

# Mutational Signatures in Cancer

López-Bigas's group has contributed to the analysis of mutational
signatures — patterns of somatic mutations that reflect the mutational
processes active in a tumor (APOBEC, MMR deficiency, UV, etc.).
Identifying signatures is essential for interpreting individual mutations.

**Key contributions:**
- Analysis of mutational signatures across TCGA cancer types
- Integration of signatures with driver mutation analysis
- Signature-aware driver gene discovery
- Tools for signature decomposition

**Reference:** Alexandrov et al., Nature (2013). PMID: 23945592

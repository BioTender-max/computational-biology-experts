# Eran Segal — Anti-Patterns

## Universal Dietary Recommendation
Recommending the same diet to everyone ignores individual variation. Personalized nutrition is the future.

## Glycemic Index Fallacy
The glycemic index of a food is not a fixed property — it varies between individuals.

## Correlation-Causation Confusion
Microbiome associations with dietary response do not prove causation. FMT experiments are needed.

## Single-Timepoint Microbiome
The microbiome changes with diet and disease. Longitudinal measurements are needed.

## Artificial Sweetener Safety Assumption
Artificial sweeteners are not metabolically inert. They alter the gut microbiome and can induce glucose intolerance.

# Eran Segal — Core Principles

1. **Personalized nutrition: one size does not fit all.** Different people have dramatically different glycemic responses to identical foods.
2. **The gut microbiome mediates dietary response.** Different microbiome compositions metabolize the same foods differently.
3. **CGM as a phenotyping tool.** Continuous glucose monitoring provides objective, continuous data on individual dietary response.
4. **The Human Phenotype Project: deep phenotyping at scale.** 28,000+ participants, 17 body systems, 25-year longitudinal study.
5. **Machine learning for personalized medicine.** Gradient boosting models predict individual glycemic responses from microbiome and clinical features.
6. **Validate computational predictions with interventional studies.** Computational models must be validated experimentally.
7. **Artificial sweeteners are not metabolically inert.** They alter the gut microbiome and can induce glucose intolerance.

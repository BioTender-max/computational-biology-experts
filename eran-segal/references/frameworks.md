# Eran Segal — Conceptual Frameworks

## Framework 1: Personalized Glycemic Response Prediction
**Problem**: Blood glucose levels after meals vary dramatically between individuals eating identical foods. Universal dietary recommendations ignore this variation.

**Study design**:
- 800-person cohort; continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) every 5 minutes for a week
- 46,898 meals measured
- Multi-omic profiling: blood parameters, dietary habits, anthropometrics, physical activity, gut microbiota

**Algorithm**: Machine learning integrating all measured features to predict personalized postprandial glycemic responses.

**Validation**: Blinded RCT in 100 new participants; personalized diets significantly lowered postprandial responses and altered gut microbiota configuration.

**Key insight**: "The best diet for humans does not exist. Our responses to food are personal, so our dietary advice must also be personal."

## Framework 2: Microbiome as Causal Factor
**Insight**: The gut microbiome is not just a correlate of health — it is a causal factor.

**Evidence**:
- Artificial sweeteners alter microbiome composition → glucose intolerance (Nature 2014)
- Microbiome transplants from lean to overweight individuals → weight reduction (animal models)
- Red meat → microbiome-mediated conversion → artery-clogging molecules

**Human Phenotype Project (10K Initiative)**:
- 10,000+ individuals followed longitudinally
- Comprehensive profiling: lifestyle, disease history, microbiome, genomics, metabolomics, wearables
- Goal: build holistic AI models for personalized health

## Framework 3: Nucleosome Positioning Code
**Insight**: Nucleosome positions along the genome are encoded in the DNA sequence.

**Key features**:
- Poly(dA:dT) tracts disfavor nucleosome formation
- GC-rich sequences favor nucleosome formation
- Nucleosome affinity landscape assists in directing TFs to their appropriate sites

**Application**: Predicting nucleosome positions genome-wide from DNA sequence; understanding how chromatin structure shapes gene expression.

---

## Framework 4: Quantitative Models of Gene Regulation
**Insight**: Gene regulation can be modeled quantitatively from DNA sequence.

**Method**: High-throughput measurements of thousands of systematically designed promoters; machine learning to infer gene regulatory logic.

**Applications**:
- Predicting expression patterns from regulatory sequence in Drosophila segmentation (Nature 2008)
- Inferring gene regulatory logic from high-throughput measurements (Nature Biotechnology 2012)
- Understanding design principles of regulatory sequences (Nature Reviews Genetics 2014)

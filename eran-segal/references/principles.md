# Eran Segal — Core Principles

## 1. Universal dietary recommendations are inherently flawed
"The best diet for humans does not exist. Our responses to food are personal." The assumption that one diet works for everyone ignores the enormous individual variation in genetics, microbiome, and lifestyle.

## 2. Individual variation is the signal
"High variability in the response to identical meals, suggesting that universal dietary recommendations may have limited utility." Individual variation is not noise to be averaged away — it is the signal that enables personalized medicine.

## 3. Large cohorts with continuous monitoring
800-person cohort; 46,898 meals; CGM every 5 minutes for a week. Individual data points are too noisy; large cohorts with continuous monitoring reveal robust patterns.

## 4. Integrate multi-omic data
No single data type is sufficient. Combine genomics, microbiome, metabolomics, clinical data, dietary logs, and wearable data to build predictive models.

## 5. Machine learning for personalized prediction
"Advanced machine learning algorithms to automatically search for rules that predict personalized glucose responses." The goal is not to describe associations but to predict individual responses and design personalized interventions.

## 6. Validate with RCTs
"A blinded randomized controlled dietary intervention based on this algorithm resulted in significantly lower postprandial responses." Computational predictions must be validated with RCTs.

## 7. Microbiome as causal factor
"I believe that the microbiome can cause obesity. We've shown that in animal models." The microbiome is not just a correlate of health — it is a causal factor.

## 8. Longitudinal perspective
Human Phenotype Project: 10,000+ individuals followed over years. Short-term studies miss the most important patterns.

## 9. Gene regulation from DNA sequence
Nucleosome positioning code; quantitative models of transcriptional behavior. Gene regulation can be modeled quantitatively from DNA sequence.

## 10. Personalized medicine as the goal
"We aim to develop personalized nutrition and personalized medicine." All computational work is in service of this translational goal.

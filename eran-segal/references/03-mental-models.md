# Eran Segal — Mental Models

## Individual Variation as the Key Signal
Most nutrition research focuses on average effects. But the most important signal is individual variation — why do different people respond differently to the same food? The microbiome, genetics, and lifestyle are the key sources of this variation.

## The Microbiome as a Mediator
The microbiome mediates the relationship between diet and health outcomes. By measuring the microbiome alongside dietary intake and health outcomes, we can understand the mechanisms of individual variation.

## CGM as Ground Truth
Self-reported dietary intake is notoriously inaccurate. CGM provides objective, continuous data on how each individual responds to each food. This ground truth data enables the development of personalized dietary recommendations.

## The HPP as a 25-Year Investment
The Human Phenotype Project is a 25-year investment in deep phenotyping. The value of longitudinal data compounds over time — the longer the study, the more disease trajectories can be identified.

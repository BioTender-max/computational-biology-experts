# Quotes & Perspectives — Anshul Kundaje

## On Regulatory Genomics
*"We want to decode the functional language encoded in DNA — the grammar of gene regulation."*

## On Interpretability
*"Interpretability is as important as predictive accuracy. A model that predicts but cannot explain is less useful than one that reveals mechanism."*

## On Base-Pair Resolution
*"Base-pair resolution is where the regulatory logic lives."*

## On ENCODE
*"ENCODE and Roadmap Epigenomics provide the training data needed to build generalizable models of gene regulation."*

## On Variant Interpretation
*"The ultimate test of a regulatory genomics model is whether it can predict the effects of genetic variants on gene expression and disease."*

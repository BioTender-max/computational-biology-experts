# Rob Knight — Key Quotes

"The microbiome is the fourth organ. We've been ignoring it for centuries, but it's as important as the heart or the liver."

"QIIME democratized microbiome science. Before QIIME, you needed a bioinformatics team. After QIIME, any biologist could do it."

"The Earth Microbiome Project showed us that microbial communities are structured by their environment."

"The American Gut Project proved that citizen science can generate high-quality microbiome data at scale."

"Science doesn't have to happen only in academic labs."

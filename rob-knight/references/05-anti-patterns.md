# Rob Knight — Anti-Patterns

## OTU vs. ASV Confusion
OTUs (97% similarity clusters) are being replaced by ASVs (exact sequence variants). Use ASVs for new studies.

## Correlation vs. Causation Fallacy
Microbiome associations with disease do not prove causation. FMT experiments are needed.

## Single-Timepoint Limitation
The microbiome is dynamic. Longitudinal studies are needed for mechanistic insights.

## 16S Resolution Limit
16S rRNA cannot resolve species or strains. Use shotgun metagenomics for strain-level resolution.

## Rarefaction Debate
Without rarefaction, samples with more reads appear more diverse. Always rarefy before diversity analysis.

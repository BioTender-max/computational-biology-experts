# Rob Knight — Mental Models

## The Microbiome as an Ecosystem
The gut microbiome is an ecosystem with its own ecology — species compete for resources, cooperate in metabolic networks, and respond to environmental perturbations. Ecological principles (diversity, stability, resilience) apply to the microbiome.

## UniFrac as Phylogenetic Distance
UniFrac measures the fraction of the phylogenetic tree unique to one sample. Two samples with similar taxa are close in UniFrac space; two samples with distantly related taxa are far apart. This phylogenetic distance is more biologically meaningful than simple presence/absence metrics.

## The EMP as a Reference Map
The Earth Microbiome Project provides a reference map of microbial diversity across environments. New samples can be placed in this reference map to understand their ecological context.

## Citizen Science as a Scaling Strategy
The American Gut Project demonstrated that citizen science can generate high-quality microbiome data at scale. Participants collect their own samples, enabling studies with thousands of participants at a fraction of the cost of traditional clinical studies.

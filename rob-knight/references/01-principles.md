# Rob Knight — Core Principles

1. **The microbiome as a fourth organ.** The gut microbiome actively influences host metabolism, immunity, and behavior.
2. **Diversity as the key metric.** Alpha and beta diversity are the primary metrics for characterizing microbiome composition.
3. **Scale enables discovery.** Thousands of samples are needed to identify robust microbiome-health associations.
4. **Standardization enables comparison.** QIIME provides standardized, reproducible pipelines for 16S rRNA analysis.
5. **Open science accelerates discovery.** EMP and AGP are open-access resources.
6. **Phylogenetic metrics are more informative.** UniFrac accounts for evolutionary relationships between taxa.
7. **Metadata is as important as sequence data.** Biological interpretation depends on metadata quality.

# Albert-László Barabási — Key Sources

## Foundational Papers
- Barabási AL, Albert R. Emergence of scaling in random networks. *Science*. 1999;286(5439):509-512. (30,000+ citations)
- Barabási AL, Gulbahce N, Loscalzo J. Network medicine: a network-based approach to human disease. *Nat Rev Genet*. 2011;12(1):56-68.
- Liu YY, Slotine JJ, Barabási AL. Controllability of complex networks. *Nature*. 2011;473(7346):167-173.
- Guney E, Menche J, Vidal M, Barabási AL. Network-based in silico drug efficacy screening. *Nat Commun*. 2016;7:10331.

## Books
- Barabási AL. *Linked: The New Science of Networks*. Perseus, 2002.
- Barabási AL. *Network Science*. Cambridge University Press, 2016. (Free online: networksciencebook.com)
- Barabási AL. *The Formula: The Universal Laws of Success*. Little Brown, 2018.
- Barabási AL, Wang D. *The Science of Science*. Cambridge University Press, 2021.

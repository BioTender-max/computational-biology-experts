## Landmark papers

15. **Kuhlman et al. (2003)** — Design of a novel globular protein fold with atomic-level accuracy.
    *Science.* The Top7 paper: first protein with a completely new fold designed from scratch.

16. **Watson et al. (2023)** — De novo design of protein structure and function with RFdiffusion.
    *Nature.* DOI: 10.1038/s41586-023-06415-8

17. **Dauparas et al. (2022)** — Robust deep learning-based protein sequence design using ProteinMPNN.
    *Science.* The ProteinMPNN paper.

18. **Huang, Boyken & Baker (2016)** — The coming of age of de novo protein design.
    *Nature.* DOI: 10.1038/nature19946. Review of the field's maturation.

19. **Baker (2019)** — What has de novo protein design taught us about protein folding and biophysics?
    *Protein Science.* DOI: 10.1002/pro.3588. Baker's reflective essay on lessons from design.

20. **Baker & Church (2024)** — Protein design meets biosecurity.
    *Science.* DOI: 10.1126/science.ado1671. On responsible innovation in protein design.

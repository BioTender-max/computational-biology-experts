# Heuristics — Elhanan Borenstein

## Practical Rules for Computational Microbiome Research

1. **Use metabolic modeling**: Metabolic models reveal the functional interactions between species that compositional analysis misses.

2. **Integrate multiple omics layers**: No single omics layer captures the full picture. Integrate metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, and metabolomics.

3. **Consider dietary context**: Diet is the primary driver of microbiome composition and function. Always consider dietary context.

4. **Use perturbation experiments**: Dietary interventions, antibiotics, and probiotics reveal microbiome function that observational studies miss.

5. **Validate computationally predicted interactions**: Predicted species interactions must be validated experimentally.

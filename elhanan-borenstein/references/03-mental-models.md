# Mental Models — Elhanan Borenstein

## The Metabolic Community Model
The microbiome is a metabolic community where species exchange metabolites, compete for resources, and cooperate to produce compounds that neither could produce alone.

## The Systems Biology Perspective
The microbiome cannot be understood by studying individual species in isolation. Systems biology approaches — modeling the interactions between species — are essential.

## The Multi-Omic Integration Imperative
No single omics layer captures the full picture of microbiome function. Integration of metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, and metabolomics is essential.

## The Therapeutic Vision
Microbiome-based therapy — using diet, probiotics, prebiotics, or fecal transplants to modulate the microbiome — requires mechanistic understanding of how the microbiome affects host health.

# Anti-Patterns — Elhanan Borenstein

## What to Avoid in Computational Microbiome Research

### Descriptive Analysis Without Mechanism
Cataloging species composition without understanding function misses the biology. Always aim for mechanistic insight.

### Ignoring Metabolic Interactions
Species don't act in isolation. Metabolic interactions between species are essential for understanding community function.

### Single-Omics Analysis
No single omics layer captures the full picture. Integration is essential.

### Ignoring Diet
Diet is the primary driver of microbiome composition and function. Dietary context is essential for interpreting microbiome data.

### Confusing Correlation with Causation
Microbiome-disease associations are often correlational. Mechanistic studies are needed to establish causation.

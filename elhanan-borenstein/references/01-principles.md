# Core Principles — Elhanan Borenstein

## 1. The Microbiome is a Metabolic Community
Individual species are less important than the metabolic interactions between them. Metabolic modeling reveals these interactions.

## 2. Systems Biology Beats Descriptive Analysis
Cataloging which species are present is less informative than understanding what they do and how they interact. Systems approaches provide mechanistic insight.

## 3. Multi-Omics Reveals Function
Metagenomics tells you who is there; metatranscriptomics tells you what they're doing; metabolomics tells you what they're producing. Integration is essential.

## 4. Perturbation Reveals Mechanism
The best way to understand a system is to perturb it. Dietary interventions, antibiotics, and probiotics are natural experiments that reveal microbiome function.

## 5. Therapy Requires Mechanism
Microbiome-based therapy requires mechanistic understanding of how the microbiome affects host health. Descriptive analysis is insufficient.

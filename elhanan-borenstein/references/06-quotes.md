# Quotes & Perspectives — Elhanan Borenstein

## On Systems Biology
*"The microbiome is a complex system that cannot be understood by studying individual species in isolation."*

## On Research Approach
*"Systems biology approaches are essential for understanding microbiome function — metabolic modeling, network theory, and multi-omic integration."*

## On Therapeutic Goals
*"Our research ultimately aims to provide a systems-based understanding of the microbiome in health and in disease and to inform microbiome-based therapy."*

## On Multi-Omics
*"Metagenomics tells you who is there; metatranscriptomics tells you what they're doing; metabolomics tells you what they're producing. Integration is essential."*

# Frameworks — Elhanan Borenstein

## Community Metabolic Modeling
1. **Species identification**: Identify microbial species from metagenomic data
2. **Metabolic reconstruction**: Reconstruct metabolic networks for each species
3. **Community model**: Combine species models into a community model
4. **Flux balance analysis**: Compute metabolic fluxes in the community
5. **Prediction**: Predict metabolite production and species interactions

## Multi-Omic Microbiome Integration
1. **Metagenomics**: Identify species and genes
2. **Metatranscriptomics**: Measure gene expression
3. **Metabolomics**: Measure metabolite profiles
4. **Integration**: Correlate omics layers to identify functional modules
5. **Disease association**: Link functional modules to disease phenotypes

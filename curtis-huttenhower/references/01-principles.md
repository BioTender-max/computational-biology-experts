# Curtis Huttenhower — Core Principles

1. **Function over taxonomy.** What are the microbes doing? Functional metagenomics is essential.
2. **The microbiome as a molecular epidemiology target.** MWAS identifies microbial taxa and functions associated with disease.
3. **Reproducibility requires standardized software.** bioBakery provides standardized, well-tested, open-source tools.
4. **The microbiome-immunity interface.** Dysbiosis is associated with autoimmune diseases and IBD.
5. **Public health is the ultimate goal.** Microbiome science must translate to population-level interventions.
6. **Multi-omics for mechanism.** Integrate metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, and metabolomics.
7. **Longitudinal studies for dynamics.** Cross-sectional studies miss temporal dynamics.

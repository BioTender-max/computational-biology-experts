# Curtis Huttenhower — Mental Models

## Function as the Key to Mechanism
Taxonomy tells you who is there; function tells you what they are doing. The same taxonomic composition can have very different functional profiles. Function is the key to understanding mechanism.

## The Microbiome as a Modifiable Risk Factor
Unlike the genome, the microbiome can be changed by diet, antibiotics, and probiotics. This makes it a particularly attractive target for therapeutic intervention.

## Compositional Data as a Statistical Challenge
Metagenomics data is compositional — the relative abundance of each taxon depends on all other taxa. Standard statistical tests are inappropriate. Methods designed for compositional data (ALDEx2, ANCOM, MaAsLin2) are required.

## The bioBakery as a Reproducibility Infrastructure
bioBakery provides the infrastructure for reproducible metagenomics analysis. Every tool is open source, well-tested, and documented. Reproducibility is not just a scientific virtue — it is a practical necessity.

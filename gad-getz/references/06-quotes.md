# Quotes & Perspectives — Gad Getz

## On Cancer Genomics
*"To find driver mutations, you must first model the background rate of passenger mutations."*

## On Statistical Rigor
*"Cancer genomics is fundamentally a statistical problem — distinguishing signal from noise in a sea of mutations."*

## On TCGA
*"The TCGA has generated the most comprehensive catalog of cancer mutations ever assembled, and we are still learning from it."*

## On MuTect
*"Tumor-normal pairing is fundamental to somatic mutation detection. MuTect's Bayesian model was designed to maximize sensitivity while controlling false positives."*

## On the Future
*"Proteogenomics — integrating genomics with proteomics — is revealing the functional consequences of cancer mutations that genomics alone cannot see."*

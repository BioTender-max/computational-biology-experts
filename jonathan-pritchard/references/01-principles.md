# Jonathan Pritchard — Core Principles

1. **Population structure is everywhere and must be modeled** — Every human population has complex ancestry; ignoring it leads to spurious associations and incorrect inferences.
2. **Complex traits are polygenic — more than we thought** — The omnigenic model argues that essentially all genes expressed in a relevant tissue contribute to complex trait variation.
3. **Polygenic adaptation is the dominant mode of recent human evolution** — Recent evolution is driven by subtle shifts in allele frequencies at thousands of loci, not hard sweeps.
4. **Gene regulation is the key to complex trait biology** — Most GWAS hits are in noncoding regions and act by regulating gene expression.
5. **Statistical rigor is necessary but not sufficient** — Rigorous methods must be grounded in biological models.
6. **Human evolutionary history is written in population structure** — Patterns of genetic variation record the history of migrations, bottlenecks, and admixture.
7. **Replication is essential in human genetics** — The history of the field is littered with false positives; always replicate.

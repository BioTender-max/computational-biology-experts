## 3 Landmark Papers

1. **Pritchard JK, Stephens M, Donnelly P (2000).** Inference of population structure using multilocus genotype data. *Genetics*, 155(2):945–959. — Introduced STRUCTURE; >40,000 citations; foundational tool for population genetics and GWAS.

2. **Boyle EA, Li YI, Pritchard JK (2017).** An expanded view of complex traits: from polygenic to omnigenic. *Cell*, 169(7):1177–1186. — Introduced the omnigenic model; transformed how the field thinks about complex trait architecture.

3. **Pritchard JK, Pickrell JK, Coop G (2010).** The genetics of human adaptation: hard sweeps, soft sweeps, and polygenic adaptation. *Current Biology*, 20(4):R208–R215. — Established polygenic adaptation as the dominant mode of recent human evolution.

# Heuristics — Wouter Meuleman

## Practical Rules for Regulatory Genomics

1. **Use the DHS index**: The comprehensive index of human regulatory elements is the starting point for regulatory genomics analysis.

2. **Consider cell-type specificity**: Regulatory elements are cell-type-specific. Always consider which cell types are relevant for your question.

3. **Validate generative models experimentally**: Synthetic regulatory elements must be validated in reporter assays.

4. **Use visualization**: Large-scale epigenomics data requires visualization to be interpretable. Invest in good visualization tools.

5. **Integrate with genetic data**: Regulatory maps are most powerful when integrated with GWAS and eQTL data.

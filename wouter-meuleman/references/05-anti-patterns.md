# Anti-Patterns — Wouter Meuleman

## What to Avoid in Regulatory Genomics

### Ignoring Data Scale
Modern epigenomics datasets are too large for manual analysis. Computational methods are essential.

### Treating Regulatory Elements as Binary
Regulatory activity is quantitative and context-dependent. Binary classifications miss important information.

### Ignoring Cell-Type Specificity
Regulatory elements are cell-type-specific. Tissue-agnostic analysis misses important biology.

### Not Validating Generative Models
Synthetic regulatory elements must be validated experimentally. Computational predictions alone are insufficient.

### Ignoring Variant Context
The functional consequences of non-coding variants depend on the regulatory context. Always consider cell-type-specific regulatory maps.

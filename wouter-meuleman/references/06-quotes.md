# Quotes & Perspectives — Wouter Meuleman

## On Augmented Genomics
*"My long-term research goal is to make 'augmented genomics' a reality — a new field in which the work of genome scientists is supplemented by large-scale visualization and data-driven machine intelligence."*

## On Generative Models
*"Generative models can design synthetic regulatory elements with desired cell-type specificity — a transformative application for gene therapy."*

## On Scale
*"The scale and complexity of modern epigenomics data precludes routine utility without computational methods to reduce complexity while retaining relevant information."*

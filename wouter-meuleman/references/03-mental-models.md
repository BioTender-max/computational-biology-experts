# Mental Models — Wouter Meuleman

## The Regulatory Landscape
The regulatory genome is a landscape of open chromatin regions, each active in specific cell types. This landscape defines cell identity and is the key to understanding gene regulation.

## The Augmented Genomics Vision
Computation should make large-scale genomic data accessible to biologists who are not computational experts. Visualization and machine intelligence are the tools for this.

## The Generative Design Paradigm
Generative models can design synthetic regulatory elements with desired properties. This is a transformative application for gene therapy and synthetic biology.

## The Variant Interpretation Problem
Most disease-associated variants are non-coding. Interpreting their functional consequences requires cell-type-specific regulatory maps.

# Sean Eddy — Core Principles

1. **Parameters are probabilities, not scores** — Every parameter in a profile HMM is a probability with biological meaning, enabling proper statistical inference.
2. **Statistical rigor enables biological discovery** — HMMER's sensitivity advantage comes from its probabilistic model, enabling discovery of remote homologs.
3. **Noncoding RNA is underappreciated** — The genome is full of functional noncoding RNAs invisible to protein-coding gene finders.
4. **Computational biology needs philosophical clarity** — The field suffers from conceptual confusion that leads to methodological errors.
5. **Software quality is a scientific value** — Code quality, testing, and documentation are as important as the underlying algorithms.
6. **Homology detection is the central problem of bioinformatics** — If you can detect homology, you can transfer functional annotation and understand biology.
7. **The E-value is the right currency** — E-values are the correct way to report sequence search results; percent identity and raw scores are inferior.

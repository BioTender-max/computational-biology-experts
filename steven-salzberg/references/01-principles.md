# Steven Salzberg — Core Principles

1. **Tools must work on real data, not just benchmarks** — Benchmarks don't capture all properties of real data; validate on real data from the organism of interest.
2. **Genome annotation is never finished** — Every annotation is a work in progress; new data and methods continuously improve annotations.
3. **RNA-seq analysis requires careful attention to splicing** — The human genome has ~200,000 distinct transcripts; tools must handle alternative splicing.
4. **Metagenomics requires careful attention to contamination** — Always check for human DNA and reagent contamination in environmental samples.
5. **Methodological rigor is not optional** — The history of bioinformatics is full of tools that were widely used but methodologically flawed.
6. **Education and communication are scientific responsibilities** — Scientists have a responsibility to communicate clearly and correct misinformation.
7. **Mentoring is a multiplier** — Salzberg's doctoral students (Trapnell, Langmead, Phillippy, Schatz) have each made major contributions to the field.

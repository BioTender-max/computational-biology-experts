# Swiss-Prot — The Gold Standard Protein Database

Swiss-Prot (1986) is a manually curated protein sequence database created
by Amos Bairoch. It is the most cited database in biology and the gold
standard for protein annotation. Every entry is manually reviewed by an
expert curator and cites primary literature.

**Key features:**
- Manual expert curation for every entry
- Evidence codes for every annotation
- Controlled vocabulary for function, location, PTMs, disease
- >570,000 manually reviewed entries in UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot

**Reference:** Bairoch & Apweiler, Nucleic Acids Research (2000). PMID: 10592178

# UniProt — Universal Protein Resource

UniProt (2002) is the world's largest protein sequence and annotation
database, created by merging Swiss-Prot (SIB), TrEMBL (EBI), and PIR-PSD.
It contains >250 million protein sequences with expert-curated annotations
for >570,000 entries (UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot).

**Architecture:**
- UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot: manually reviewed (gold standard)
- UniProtKB/TrEMBL: computationally annotated (unreviewed)
- UniRef: clustered sequences at 100%, 90%, 50% identity
- UniParc: sequence archive with cross-references

**Reference:** UniProt Consortium, Nucleic Acids Research (2023). PMID: 36408920

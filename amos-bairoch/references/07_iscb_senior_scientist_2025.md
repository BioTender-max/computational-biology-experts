# ISCB Senior Scientist Award 2025

Amos Bairoch received the ISCB Senior Scientist Award in 2025 — one of
the last honors he received before his death on November 29, 2025. The
award recognized his lifetime contributions to bioinformatics databases,
biocuration, and the infrastructure of molecular biology.

**Career highlights:**
- Creator of Swiss-Prot, UniProt, PROSITE, ExPASy, Cellosaurus
- Co-founder, SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics
- Professor, University of Geneva
- Died November 29, 2025, aged 69

**Reference:** ISCB Senior Scientist Award announcement (2025);
SIB memorial notice (December 2025)

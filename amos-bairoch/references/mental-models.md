# Cellosaurus — Cell Line Knowledge Resource

Cellosaurus (2012) is a controlled vocabulary and knowledge resource for
cell lines. Created by Bairoch, it addresses the reproducibility crisis
caused by misidentified and cross-contaminated cell lines. Each entry
includes origin, species, disease, cross-references, and publications.

**Key features:**
- >170,000 cell line entries
- Cross-references to 100+ databases
- Misidentified cell line tracking
- STR profile data for authentication

**Reference:** Bairoch, Journal of Immunological Sciences (2018). PMID: 29805321

# Biocuration as a Scientific Discipline

Bairoch is widely regarded as the father of biocuration — the discipline
of extracting, organizing, and standardizing biological knowledge from
primary literature into computable databases. He argued throughout his
career that biocuration is a scientific contribution equal to any
experimental paper.

**Key contributions:**
- Defined biocuration as a scientific discipline
- Co-founded the International Society for Biocuration (ISB)
- Advocated for recognition of curators as scientists
- Trained a generation of biocurators at SIB

**Reference:** Bairoch, Database (2009). PMID: 20157488

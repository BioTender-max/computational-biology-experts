# PROSITE — Protein Domain and Motif Database

PROSITE (1988) is a database of protein families, domains, and functional
sites, represented as patterns (regular expressions) and profiles. Created
by Bairoch, it was the first systematic approach to protein family
classification and is still maintained by the SIB.

**Key features:**
- Regular expression patterns for functional sites
- Profile HMMs for protein domains
- Cross-linked to Swiss-Prot/UniProt
- Foundation for protein family classification

**Reference:** Hulo et al., Nucleic Acids Research (2006). PMID: 16381852

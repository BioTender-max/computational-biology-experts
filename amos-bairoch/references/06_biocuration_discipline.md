# Biocuration as a Scientific Discipline

Bairoch is widely regarded as the father of biocuration — the discipline
of extracting, organizing, and standardizing biological knowledge from
primary literature into computable databases. He argued throughout his
career that biocuration is a scientific contribution equal to any
experimental paper.

**Key contributions:**
- Defined biocuration as a scientific discipline
- Co-founded the International Society for Biocuration (ISB)
- Advocated for recognition of curators as scientists
- Trained a generation of biocurators at SIB

**Reference:** Bairoch, Database (2009). PMID: 20157488

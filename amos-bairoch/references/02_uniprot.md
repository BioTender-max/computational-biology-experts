# UniProt — Universal Protein Resource

UniProt (2002) is the world's largest protein sequence and annotation
database, created by merging Swiss-Prot (SIB), TrEMBL (EBI), and PIR-PSD.
It contains >250 million protein sequences with expert-curated annotations
for >570,000 entries (UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot).

**Architecture:**
- UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot: manually reviewed (gold standard)
- UniProtKB/TrEMBL: computationally annotated (unreviewed)
- UniRef: clustered sequences at 100%, 90%, 50% identity
- UniParc: sequence archive with cross-references

**Reference:** UniProt Consortium, Nucleic Acids Research (2023). PMID: 36408920

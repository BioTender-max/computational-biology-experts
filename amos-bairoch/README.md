# Amos Bairoch

**SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics · University of Geneva**
*(1956–2025)*

Amos Bairoch (1956–2025) was a Swiss bioinformatician at the SIB Swiss
Institute of Bioinformatics and the University of Geneva. He created Swiss-Prot
(1986), PROSITE (1988), ENZYME (1990), ExPASy (1993), UniProt (2002, with EBI
and PIR), neXtProt (2011), and Cellosaurus (2012). Swiss-Prot and UniProt are
the most cited databases in biology; UniProt contains >250 million protein
sequences. He co-founded the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics and is
widely regarded as the father of biocuration. He received the ISCB Senior
Scientist Award (2025) — one of the last honors before his death on
November 29, 2025.

## Key Contributions

- **Swiss-Prot** (1986) — gold standard manually curated protein database
- **PROSITE** (1988) — protein domain and motif database
- **ENZYME** (1990) — enzyme nomenclature database
- **ExPASy** (1993) — first integrated bioinformatics web portal
- **UniProt** (2002) — universal protein resource; >250M sequences
- **Cellosaurus** (2012) — controlled vocabulary for cell lines

## Awards & Recognition

- ISCB Senior Scientist Award (2025)
- Co-founder, SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics
- Father of biocuration
- Died November 29, 2025, aged 69

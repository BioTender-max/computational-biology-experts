## Papers

11. **"The Human Cell Atlas"**
    Regev et al., *eLife*, 2017
    URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5762154/
    Significance: Founding white paper for the Human Cell Atlas project

12. **"The Human Cell Atlas: towards a first draft atlas"**
    Rood, Wynne, Regev et al., *Nature*, 2024
    URL: https://www.nature.com/collections/jccbbdahji
    Significance: Progress report on the Human Cell Atlas

13. **"A cell atlas foundation model for scalable search of similar human cells" (SCimilarity)**
    Heimberg, Kuo, Regev et al., *Nature*, 2024
    URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08411-y
    Significance: 23.4M-cell atlas; foundation model for cell similarity search

14. **Drop-Seq: "Highly Parallel Genome-wide Expression Profiling of Individual Cells Using Nanoliter Droplets"**
    Macosko, Basu, Satija, ..., Regev, McCarroll. *Cell*, 2015
    Significance: Massively parallel scRNA-seq; reduced cost to pennies per cell

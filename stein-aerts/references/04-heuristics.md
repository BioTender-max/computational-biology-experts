# Heuristics — Stein Aerts

## Practical Rules for Regulatory Genomics

1. **Use single-cell data**: Bulk epigenomics misses cell-type-specific regulatory programs; single-cell ATAC-seq is essential.

2. **Combine SCENIC and cisTopic**: SCENIC infers GRNs from RNA; cisTopic identifies regulatory programs from ATAC. Together they provide a complete picture.

3. **Train deep learning models on large datasets**: Deep learning models for enhancer prediction require large, diverse training datasets.

4. **Validate with reporter assays**: Computational predictions of enhancer activity must be tested with massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs).

5. **Use Drosophila for mechanistic validation**: The fly's genetic tools enable experimental validation of regulatory hypotheses.

6. **Design synthetic enhancers**: Deep learning models can be used to design synthetic enhancers with desired cell-type specificity — a key application for gene therapy.

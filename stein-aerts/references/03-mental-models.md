# Mental Models — Stein Aerts

## The Regulatory Code
Gene regulation is encoded in DNA sequence — specifically in enhancers, which contain binding sites for transcription factors. The "regulatory code" is the set of rules that determine which TFs bind which enhancers in which cell types.

## The Cell Identity Landscape
Cell types exist in a landscape defined by their regulatory programs. SCENIC maps this landscape by identifying the TF regulons that define each cell type.

## The Sequence-to-Function Map
Deep learning models learn a mapping from DNA sequence to regulatory activity. This map captures the regulatory grammar — the combinatorial logic of TF binding.

## The Fly-Human Continuum
Regulatory principles are conserved across evolution. Insights from Drosophila often generalize to humans, and the fly's genetic tractability enables experimental validation that is difficult in human cells.

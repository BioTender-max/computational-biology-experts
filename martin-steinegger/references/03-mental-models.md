# Martin Steinegger — Mental Models

## Speed as a Scientific Enabler
Speed is not just about convenience — it enables entirely new scientific questions. When you can search a billion sequences in hours, you can annotate entire metagenomes, cluster all known proteins, and compare entire proteomes.

## Accessibility as a Scientific Multiplier
When a tool is accessible to all researchers (not just well-resourced labs), the number of scientific discoveries it enables multiplies. ColabFold democratized protein structure prediction, enabling thousands of labs to predict structures that would otherwise be inaccessible.

## Structure as a More Conserved Language
Protein structure is more conserved than sequence. Two proteins with <20% sequence identity can have nearly identical structures and functions. Foldseek searches in structure space, finding homologs that sequence-based methods miss.

## Linear Scaling as a Paradigm Shift
Quadratic algorithms become intractable at scale. Linear algorithms enable analysis of datasets that were previously computationally intractable. Linclust's linear scaling is a paradigm shift for sequence clustering.

# Martin Steinegger — Core Principles

1. **Speed is not a luxury — it's a scientific necessity.** 10,000x faster than BLAST enables entirely new scientific questions.
2. **Accessibility enables discovery.** ColabFold democratized protein structure prediction.
3. **The structure universe is searchable.** Foldseek enables fast search of the AlphaFold database.
4. **Linear-time algorithms change what's possible.** Linclust enables clustering of billions of sequences.
5. **Open source accelerates science.** All tools are freely available with web services.
6. **Non-traditional paths lead to innovation.** Leaving school early and working in industry provided a different perspective.
7. **Protein-level assembly recovers more.** Plass assembles at the protein level, recovering sequences that nucleotide assemblers miss.

# Michael Schatz — Core Principles

1. **Long reads reveal what short reads miss** — Short reads miss structural variants; long reads can span them and reveal them directly.
2. **Structural variants are underappreciated** — They affect much larger regions of the genome than SNPs and are often more functionally important.
3. **Plant genomics is an underexplored frontier** — Plant genomes are challenging but economically important and scientifically interesting.
4. **Cloud computing enables genomics at scale** — Cloud computing enables analysis at scales impossible on local clusters.
5. **Diversity in genomics is a scientific necessity** — Most human genomics studies have been conducted in populations of European ancestry, limiting generalizability.
6. **Education and community building are scientific contributions** — Growing and diversifying the genomics data science community is as important as publishing research.
7. **The genome is not just a sequence — it is a structure** — Structural variants, chromatin organization, and 3D genome structure are as important as the linear sequence.

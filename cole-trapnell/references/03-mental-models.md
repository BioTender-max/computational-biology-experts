# Cole Trapnell — Mental Models

## Pseudotime as Biological Progression
Pseudotime is not time — it's a quantitative measure of biological progression. Cells in a differentiating population are at different stages of the same process.

## Cell Fate Decisions as Bifurcation Points
Development involves discrete fate choices. These appear as bifurcations in the trajectory. The genes differentially regulated at bifurcation points reveal the molecular logic of fate decisions.

## Combinatorial Indexing as a Scaling Strategy
Physical isolation of cells limits throughput. Combinatorial barcoding labels cells without isolation, enabling profiling of millions of cells per experiment.

## Differential Abundance Before Differential Expression
Before asking what genes are differentially expressed, ask whether there are differences in cell type abundance. Milo tests for differential abundance at the neighborhood level.

## The Experimental-Computational Integration
The best single-cell methods combine algorithmic innovation with experimental design. Understanding the experiment is essential for building good algorithms.

# Cole Trapnell — Core Principles

1. **Pseudotime orders cells along developmental trajectories.** Single-cell snapshots can be ordered to reconstruct developmental dynamics.
2. **Cell fate decisions are bifurcation points.** Development involves discrete fate choices that appear as trajectory branches.
3. **Combinatorial indexing enables massive scale.** sci-RNA-seq profiles millions of cells without physical isolation.
4. **Embryo-scale experiments reveal complete developmental programs.** Profiling entire embryos reveals the full cellular diversity of development.
5. **Computational and experimental biology must be integrated.** The best single-cell methods combine algorithmic innovation with experimental design.
6. **Open source tools accelerate the field.** Monocle, TopHat, and Cufflinks are freely available with extensive documentation.
7. **Validate trajectories with time-series data.** Pseudotime must be validated against known developmental timing.

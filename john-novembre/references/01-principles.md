# John Novembre — Core Principles

1. **Geography shapes genetics** — Human populations are geographically structured by history, geography, and culture; this structure is reflected in genetic variation.
2. **PCA is a powerful but limited tool** — PCA reveals major axes of variation without imposing a model, but can be misleading; always interpret carefully.
3. **Visualization is a scientific tool** — The right visualization communicates a complex result more powerfully than any equation.
4. **Migration surfaces reveal barriers and corridors** — EEMS estimates effective migration rates, revealing barriers to gene flow and corridors of high migration.
5. **Population genetics is historical science** — It uses present-day genetic variation to infer past events: migrations, bottlenecks, admixture.
6. **Sample composition affects PCA** — Adding or removing populations changes the orientation of axes and apparent clustering.
7. **Communicate to broad audiences** — Population genetics has implications for medicine, anthropology, history, and public policy.

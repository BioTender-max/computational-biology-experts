## 3 Landmark Papers

1. **Novembre J, Johnson T, Bryc K, et al. (2008).** Genes mirror geography within Europe. *Nature*, 456(7218):98–101. — Showed that PCA of European genetic variation reproduces the geographic map of Europe; one of the most visually compelling results in population genetics.

2. **Novembre J, Stephens M (2008).** Interpreting principal component analyses of spatial population genetic variation. *Nature Genetics*, 40(5):646–649. — Explained the mathematical basis for why genes mirror geography; essential reading for PCA interpretation.

3. **Petkova D, Novembre J, Stephens M (2016).** Visualizing spatial population structure with estimated effective migration surfaces. *Nature Genetics*, 48(1):94–100. — Introduced EEMS; enables visualization of barriers and corridors of gene flow across geography.

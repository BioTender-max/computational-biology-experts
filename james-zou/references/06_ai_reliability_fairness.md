# AI Reliability and Fairness in Medicine

Zou's group studies how AI models fail in clinical settings — distribution
shift, demographic bias, and spurious correlations. They develop methods
to detect and mitigate these failures before clinical deployment.

**Key contributions:**
- Distribution shift detection for clinical AI
- Demographic bias analysis in medical imaging AI
- Robustness benchmarks for clinical ML
- Guidelines for responsible AI deployment in medicine

**Reference:** Zou et al., Nature Medicine (2021). PMID: 33941890

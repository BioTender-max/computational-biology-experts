# Foundation Model Interpretability

Zou's group develops methods to interpret what protein language models
and genomic foundation models have learned. Using sparse autoencoders
(SAEs) and probing classifiers, they extract biological concepts encoded
in model representations — concepts that humans haven't explicitly
articulated.

**Key contributions:**
- SAEs for protein language model interpretation
- Probing classifiers for genomic foundation models
- Discovery of novel biological concepts in model representations
- Framework for human-AI collaborative knowledge extraction

**Reference:** Zou lab preprints (2024–2025)

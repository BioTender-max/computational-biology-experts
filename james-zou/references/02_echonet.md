# EchoNet — FDA-Cleared Cardiac AI

EchoNet is a deep learning system for analyzing cardiac ultrasound
(echocardiography). Developed by Zou's group at Stanford, it was the
first AI system to receive FDA clearance for echocardiography analysis.
It predicts ejection fraction, detects cardiomyopathy, and guides
clinical decisions.

**Key features:**
- Trained on 10,000+ echocardiograms from Stanford
- Predicts left ventricular ejection fraction
- FDA-cleared for clinical use
- Open-source model and dataset

**Reference:** Ouyang et al., Nature (2020). PMID: 32614981

# Trial Pathfinder — AI for Clinical Trial Design

Trial Pathfinder integrates real-world clinico-genomic data to simulate
clinical trial eligibility criteria. It identifies eligibility criteria
that exclude patients who would benefit from treatment, enabling more
inclusive and efficient clinical trial design.

**Key features:**
- Simulates trial eligibility on real-world EHR data
- Identifies overly restrictive exclusion criteria
- Validated against completed clinical trials
- Selected as Top 10 Clinical Research Achievement (2021)

**Reference:** Liu et al., Nature (2021). PMID: 34385711

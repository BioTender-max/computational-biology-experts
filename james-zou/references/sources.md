# ISCB Overton Prize 2025

James Zou received the ISCB Overton Prize in 2025, the highest honor
for early-career computational biologists. The award recognized his
contributions to ML interpretability (SHAP), clinical AI (EchoNet),
and AI-for-science (Virtual Lab).

**Career highlights at time of award:**
- Associate Professor, Stanford Biomedical Data Science
- Creator of SHAP (millions of users), EchoNet (FDA-cleared)
- Chan-Zuckerberg Investigator (×2), Sloan Fellow
- ISCB Overton Prize 2025

**Reference:** ISCB Overton Prize announcement (2025)

# Sources — James Zou

## Primary Papers

1. **Zou, J. & Schiebinger, L. (2018)**. "AI can be sexist and racist — it's time to make it fair." *Nature*. https://doi.org/10.1038/d41586-018-05707-8
   - Foundational argument for fairness in AI; identifies sources of bias and calls for de-biasing methods.

2. **Zou, J. et al. (2018)**. "A primer on deep learning in genomics." *Nature Genetics*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-018-0295-5
   - Interactive primer on deep learning applications for genome analysis; regulatory genomics, variant calling.

3. **Gupta, A. & Zou, J. (2019)**. "Feedback GAN for DNA optimizes protein functions." *Nature Machine Intelligence*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-019-0017-4
   - FBGAN: generative adversarial network for synthetic DNA sequences encoding antimicrobial peptides.

4. **Zou, J. & Schiebinger, L. (2021)**. "Ensuring that biomedical AI benefits diverse populations." *EBioMedicine*. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2021.103358
   - Framework for identifying and mitigating bias in biomedical AI across the development lifecycle.

5. **Chen, Y.T. & Zou, J. (2023)**. "GenePT: A Simple But Effective Foundation Model for Genes and Cells Built From ChatGPT." *bioRxiv*. https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.10.16.562533
   - Literature-grounded foundation model for single-cell biology using GPT-3.5 gene embeddings.

6. **Zou, J. (2024)**. "ChatGPT is transforming peer review — how can we use it responsibly?" *Nature*. https://doi.org/10.1038/d41586-024-03588-8
   - Analysis of LLM use in peer review; 57% of researchers found AI feedback helpful.

7. **Zou, J. & Topol, E. (2025)**. "The rise of agentic AI teammates in medicine." *Lancet*. https://doi.org/10.1016/s0140-6736(25)00202-8
   - Vision for autonomous AI agents as teammates in clinical medicine.

8. **Zou, J. (2014)**. "Algorithms and Models for Genome Biology." PhD Thesis, Harvard University.
   - Foundational work on machine learning applied to genomics; epigenome variation and disease.

9. **Topol, E. (2023)**. "James Zou: one of the most prolific and creative A.I. researchers in both life science and medicine." *Ground Truths*. https://erictopol.substack.com/p/james-zou-one-of-the-most-prolific

# Virtual Lab — Autonomous AI Scientist Agents

The Virtual Lab (2025) is a framework for autonomous AI scientist agents
that collaborate to design and interpret experiments. Specialized agents
(immunologist, chemist, computational biologist) work together, with a
principal investigator agent coordinating the team. Demonstrated for
nanobody discovery and COVID vaccine candidates.

**Key features:**
- Specialized agent roles with domain expertise
- Explicit disagreement protocols to prevent groupthink
- Integration with wet lab experimental results
- Demonstrated for nanobody and vaccine design

**Reference:** Swanson et al., Nature (2025)

# Trial Pathfinder — AI for Clinical Trial Design

Trial Pathfinder integrates real-world clinico-genomic data to simulate
clinical trial eligibility criteria. It identifies eligibility criteria
that exclude patients who would benefit from treatment, enabling more
inclusive and efficient clinical trial design.

**Key features:**
- Simulates trial eligibility on real-world EHR data
- Identifies overly restrictive exclusion criteria
- Validated against completed clinical trials
- Selected as Top 10 Clinical Research Achievement (2021)

**Reference:** Liu et al., Nature (2021). PMID: 34385711

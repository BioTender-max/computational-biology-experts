# James Zou

**Stanford University · Associate Professor of Biomedical Data Science**

James Zou is Associate Professor of Biomedical Data Science (and by courtesy
Computer Science and Electrical Engineering) at Stanford University, where he
leads the Stanford AI for Science Lab. His lab created SHAP (the most widely
used ML interpretability framework, used by millions of developers), EchoNet
(first FDA-cleared AI for cardiac ultrasound), Trial Pathfinder (AI for
clinical trial design), and the Virtual Lab (autonomous AI scientist agents).
He received the ISCB Overton Prize (2025), two Chan-Zuckerberg Investigator
Awards, and a Sloan Fellowship.

## Key Contributions

- **SHAP** (2017) — most widely used ML interpretability framework
- **EchoNet** (2020) — first FDA-cleared AI for cardiac ultrasound
- **Trial Pathfinder** (2021) — AI for clinical trial eligibility design
- **Virtual Lab** (2025) — autonomous AI scientist agents (Nature)
- **Foundation Model Interpretability** — SAEs for protein language models
- **AI Reliability & Fairness** — robustness and bias in clinical AI

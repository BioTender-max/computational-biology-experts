# Alkes Price — Core Principles

1. **Population stratification is the original sin of GWAS** — Confounding by ancestry was responsible for many false positives in early GWAS; PCA-based correction is now standard.
2. **LD score regression separates polygenicity from confounding** — Inflated GWAS statistics can be caused by either polygenicity or confounding; LDSC distinguishes these.
3. **Heritability is partitioned across functional categories** — Variants in functional regions are enriched for heritability; stratified LDSC quantifies this enrichment.
4. **Common variants explain substantial heritability** — Common variants collectively explain a large fraction of heritability for most complex traits.
5. **Genetic correlation reveals shared biology** — Two traits with high genetic correlation share genetic architecture and causal pathways.
6. **Functional annotation improves GWAS power** — Incorporating functional annotation improves power to detect associations and prioritize causal variants.
7. **Summary statistics enable large-scale meta-analysis** — GWAS summary statistics can be shared without individual-level data, enabling large-scale collaboration.

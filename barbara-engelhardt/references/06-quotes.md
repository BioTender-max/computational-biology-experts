# Quotes & Perspectives — Barbara Engelhardt

## On Statistical Modeling
*"The most creative statistical models are built on rigorous mathematical foundations."*

## On Uncertainty
*"Uncertainty is not a weakness of a model — it is information that should be communicated to the user."*

## On Clinical Decision-Making
*"For clinical decision-making, we need causal models, not just predictive ones."*

## On Confounders
*"Unmodeled confounders are the enemy of reproducible genomics. PEER was designed to find and remove them."*

## On the GTEx Consortium
*"GTEx revealed the tissue-specificity of genetic regulatory effects — a fundamental insight into how non-coding variants affect gene expression."*

## Signature Contributions

- **PEER**: Probabilistic latent factor model for removing confounders from gene expression data; standard in GTEx
- **GTEx Consortium**: Key computational analyses of tissue-specific gene regulation across 54 human tissues
- **GPLVM for single-cell data**: Scalable Gaussian process latent variable model for dimensionality reduction
- **RL for clinical decision support**: Reinforcement learning for ICU treatment optimization
- **Causal genomics**: Methods for predicting effects of genetic and molecular perturbations
